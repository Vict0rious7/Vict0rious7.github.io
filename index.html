Pokémon tools by Vict0rious7

Free, open-source tools for Pokémon RNG, shiny hunting, Mystery Gift events and moving your collection to Pokémon HOME.

PokeFinderPlus

Pokémon RNG tool for Gen 3 to 8, a fork of PokéFinder with FireRed/LeafGreen initial seeds and Gen 6 X/Y/ORAS support.

PokeTimerPlus

RNG timer for real hardware: EonTimer and FlowTimer in one app, with beeps that land on time.
🏠

Pokémon availability checker

Find out which Pokémon you can still bring to Pokémon HOME, and what to move before Bank closes on 25 February 2027.
🎁

NDS Mystery Gift server

Receive Mystery Gift events in Pokémon Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver on a real DS, from your own computer.

All source code is on GitHub. Found a bug? Open an issue on the project's repository.
