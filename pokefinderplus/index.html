PokeFinderPlus

Pokémon RNG tool for Gen 3 to 8, for shiny and perfect-IV Pokémon on real hardware.

PokeFinderPlus is an unofficial fork of PokéFinder by Admiral-Fish and contributors. It keeps everything PokéFinder does and adds the features below. Report bugs in this fork to its own issues page, not to the PokéFinder team.

DownloadSource on GitHub

What it adds to PokéFinder

Gen 3: FireRed and LeafGreen

  • Initial seeds in the Static and Wild searchers: each result shows the best reachable seed, its seed time, advances and total time, for the English, European, Japanese, mGBA and Switch versions. A port of Ten Lines by Lincoln-LM, with its community-farmed seed tables.
  • Calibration in the Generator: also generates from the neighbouring seeds, and a right-click on a result fills everything in.
  • "All methods" in the Generator and the Searcher, and an IV Calculator that also fills the nature, level and species filters.

Gen 4: Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold, SoulSilver

  • Seed to Time with a "Today" option: picks the seed you can still hit today without changing the console's clock.

Gen 6: X, Y, Omega Ruby, Alpha Sapphire (new, not yet confirmed on hardware)

  • Static generator, checked against 3DSRNGTool.
  • Seed Finder from the IVs of one or two Pokémon.
  • Gen 6 profiles with TSV and TRV.
  • Trainer ID and Secret ID: choose your TID/SID in X and Y, or find the SID and TSV of a new Omega Ruby or Alpha Sapphire save. Pairs with PokeTimerPlus.

Download

Get the latest version on the Releases page, under Assets.

  • Windows 10 / 11: PokeFinderPlus-windows.zip. Unzip and run PokeFinderPlus.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
  • macOS (Intel or Apple Silicon): PokeFinderPlus-macos.zip. Move the app to Applications, then right-click → Open the first time.
  • Linux: PokeFinderPlus-linux-AppImage.tar.gz, or PokeFinderPlus-linux.tar.gz (needs Qt 6).

Your PokéFinder profiles carry over. The app checks for a new version once a day.
