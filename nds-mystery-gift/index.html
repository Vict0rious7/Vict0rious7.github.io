NDS Mystery Gift server

Receive Mystery Gift events in Pokémon Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver on a real Nintendo DS, from your own computer.

A self-hosted server that answers your DS's Mystery Gift request over Wi-Fi, the way Nintendo's servers used to. No ROM patches and no changes to your cartridge or console.

Setup guideSource on GitHub

What works

  • Generation 4 games: Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver.
  • Original DS, DS Lite, DSi and 3DS (Gen 4 games use the DS connection slots).
  • Event files (.myg) from the preserved distributions, for example DLS1-Files.

What you need

  • A computer with Docker and OpenSSL.
  • A Wi-Fi access point with WEP or no security: Gen 4 games can't use WPA.
  • Your own certificate file. None is included; the setup guide explains where it goes.

How it works

Custom DNS on your DS sends Nintendo's Wi-Fi Connection addresses to your computer, which handles the connection check, the login and the three download steps of a Mystery Gift. The full explanation is in the README.
